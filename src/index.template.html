        Import your library

        Both sites give you a CSV file. Only books marked as read are imported, and duplicates are skipped.

        1. Goodreads: open Import and export and choose Export library. (Or My Books, then Import and export.)
        2. StoryGraph: open Export StoryGraph library and generate the export. (Or your profile, then Manage account.)
        3. Any other CSV with a title and an author column also works.
